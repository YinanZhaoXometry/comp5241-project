# Story contracts and feasibility gates — epic-grounded-mini-courses

SPEC-authored acceptance companion of [the epic](epic-grounded-mini-courses.md), written at its 2026-10-23 refinement. S-numbers match `tickets/tickets.toml` entry ids. UX behavior cited here lives in the adopted [experience spine](../../ux-ai-learning-platform/EXPERIENCE.md); consolidated consequences in the [PRD](../../prd-ai-learning-platform/prd-ai-learning-platform.md) §4.2/§8/§9.

## Story contracts

- **S1 — Confirmed-plan-to-generated-course tracer.** Consume epic 1's authorized exact-revision confirmed-plan handoff (epic-1 S5) through the versioned processor configuration/dispatch/accounting ports (epic-1 S3/S6) to generate one source-linked mini-course: one lesson with per-section source locations and an AI-generated/unreviewed label, plus one recall item, persisted as versioned course/objective/source lineage. Local synthetic processor fixtures drive the pipeline; live dispatch requires F-A. No supplementation, adaptation variants or full player in this entry. Checks: restart-safe generation jobs with one tracked result per command; stale/unconfirmed/deleted-plan and cross-owner denial; deletion of the root removes the generated derivative via epic-1 classes.
- **S2 — Bounded source retrieval and attributed supplementation.** Introduce the epic's bounded authoritative-source retrieval path under the architecture's fetch-boundary rules (AD-11): allowlisted public-source fetch adapter distinct from upload import, DNS/IP validation binding the actual connected address, redirect revalidation, denial of loopback/private/link-local/metadata networks and non-HTTPS/oversized responses, and identity/passage/retrieval-time/hash records. Per-source identity/location, a supplement badge separating supplements from the learner's uploads, side-by-side conflict display and unsupported-answer stopping without silent replacement or scored items. AI-proposed citations are never treated as verification; every supplement is checked against its fetched source. Live fetch requires F-B; fixture development proceeds without egress. Checks: attributed supplement with reachable location; both sources rendered on conflict; honest retryable fetch failure; zero fetch without its authorized bounded configuration and none to a private/loopback/metadata address.
- **S3 — Mini-course player shell and content-item tracking.** Extend generation from S1's single lesson to the plan's full lesson/objective set, and S1's single-lesson view to the spine's player: numbered lesson sequence, sticky sidebar collapsing under 850px, content-item tracker with explicit required mark-done and labeled non-blocking optional enrichment, honest processing/empty/version-superseded states, media floor, shared help/footer furniture. Checks: keyboard-complete at 360px and desktop with visible focus; no dwell/scroll completion tracking; "Plan confirmed — course not generated" and ≥120s processing states honest; no per-family activity interaction claimed (spine deferral to epic 6).
- **S4 — Background and goal adaptation.** Consume epic-1 S4's saved background/goal/timezone preferences so generation pitches the same objective per track — plain language, familiar examples, explicit prerequisites for non-CS beginners; application scenarios and trade-off depth for CS graduates — with identical factual claims and source links. Checks: side-by-side same-objective generation; changed preferences regenerate honestly on the next confirmed revision without rewriting earned progress; missing preferences default neutrally; unreviewed label, visible sources and error-report path present (parent R8).
- **S5 — Active recall practice set and typed feedback.** Generate and serve the practice set across the plan's objectives: recall cards withholding answer and rationale until submission; feedback blocks stating correctness, rationale, source and evidence kind; assisted practice labeled separately from unaided evidence; retries never double-count. Practice evidence is recorded typed for later epics without course-attempt semantics (those belong to epic 3). Checks: attempt-before-answer on every card including keyboard-only runs; feedback only after submission; fresh non-duplicate attempts on retry; deletion removes practice derivatives.
- **S6 — Learner-controlled why exploration with honest stopping.** Build the spine's why-stepper on lesson content: successive deeper levels addressing purpose, assumptions or trade-offs beyond restatement, each tied to visible sources including S2 supplements; stop control always visible; exhaustion stated honestly and no invented cause; depth never unlocks content or credit. Checks: two successive non-restatement levels with sources on a fixture lesson; past-evidence request states exhaustion and stops; keyboard-operable stop at every depth; why responses carry the unreviewed label and their derivative lineage.
- **S7 — Versioned provenance and downstream handoff surface.** Expose the authorized, versioned query surface over generated courses — objectives with versions, source-linked units, practice items, attempt-independent provenance — as the contract epics 3/5/6 consume, with synthetic consumer contract tests. Checks: pinned-revision reads stable across a superseding revision while new reads see the change; out-of-scope and cross-owner reads denied.
- **S8 — Generation honesty, consent denial and descendant lifecycle.** Integrate the epic's failure and lifecycle surface: honest generation errors/retries/timeouts with saved input by 120 seconds and no success-by-timeout; consent denial and processor ineligibility degrading to honest AI-unavailable states while manual work stands; generated-course derivatives — including S2 supplements and per-track adaptation variants — registered in epic-1 S8/S9/S10 export/deletion/retention classes with late-generation-vs-deletion races suppressed. Checks: generation raced against root and all-personal deletion commits nothing late and purges completely inside deadlines; exports include or honestly exclude per snapshot scope; declined consent performs zero external calls and zero supplement fetch; duplicate/retry commands yield one tracked effect; expiry clocks cover course derivatives; post-deletion provenance reads stay stable or scope-denied.
- **S9 — Refactor sweep.** Cleanup only, from completed-story plans and deferred review findings; preserve accepted UX/privacy/lifecycle/contract semantics; unchanged regressions; record a no-op if nothing is justified. Unfinished functionality needs its own entry.
- **S10 — Integrated study suite and authorized release verification.** Close the epic with integrated allowed/denied/error/accessibility/lifecycle and actual-AI study-journey checks across the Done-when paths; an explicitly authorized operator deploys/smoke-checks the selected environment only after gates pass. No deployment or paid resources authorized now; a blocked release cannot be recorded Done.

## Feasibility gates

- **F-A — Live processor eligibility (re-gate).** Epic 2 changes purpose (full generation) and may change model/endpoint/features/location/retention, so epic-1 S6's D5-compatible provider evidence does not carry over: an actually eligible processor/model/terms with retention-exception, deletion, non-training and location proof, plus account/region/budget/operator authorization for synthetic non-personal tests, is required before any live dispatch. Standard Anthropic retention does not pass. Blocks S1's live acceptance and S10; synthetic-fixture development is not blocked.
- **F-B — Bounded source-retrieval feasibility.** External authoritative-source fetch is a new egress surface: network scope, source licensing/terms, rate limits, content verification tooling and operator authorization need evidence before live fetch. Blocks S2's live acceptance; fixture development without egress is not blocked.
- **F-C — Deployment authorization.** Mirrors epic-1 F6: actual provisioning/deployment scope, region/plan/budget, operator responsibilities and managed copy/backup retention evidence require separate approval and verification before S10's deployed smoke check.
