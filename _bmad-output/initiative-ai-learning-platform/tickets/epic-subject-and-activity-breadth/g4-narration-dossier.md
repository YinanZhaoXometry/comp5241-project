## Voice/model licences (the load-bearing half for 6.6)

- Voice repository [rhasspy/piper-voices on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices)
  carries an MIT tag for the repository, but Piper's own
  [VOICES.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OHF-Voice/Piper1-GPL/main/docs/VOICES.md)
  states the real rule: "The `MODEL_CARD` file for each voice contains important licensing
  information… Some voices may have restrictive licenses, however, so please review them
  carefully!" — **engine licence and voice licence are separate admission facts.**
- The trialed voice **en_US-amy-low**: its
  [MODEL_CARD](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/resolve/main/en/en_US/amy/low/MODEL_CARD)
  says only "License: See URL" and traces to [MycroftAI/mimic3-voices](https://github.com/MycroftAI/mimic3-voices)
  (repository licence CC-BY-SA-4.0; per-voice/dataset terms not enumerated on the fetched
  page; amy is "Finetuned from U.S. English Ryan voice", and "ryan" is not among the en_US
  voices that repo lists) — **verdict: QUARANTINE-ON-DOUBT for activation.** Fine for this
  local research trial; NOT admissible for narration activation without closing the chain.
- Voice-selection input for 6.6: prefer voices whose MODEL_CARD names a dataset with clear
  terms (e.g. LJSpeech or LibriTTS-family or CMU-Arctic-derived voices) and record the chain;
  the mimic3-derived default voices are the doubtful class.

## Quality note (honestly labelled)

The agent cannot listen. Objective facts: Piper/amy produced natural-length prosody timing
(17.5 s for ~16 s of `say` audio on the same text); `say`/Samantha is Apple's premium on-device
neural voice; espeak-ng is a formant synthesizer — widely documented as robotic/intelligible
rather than natural, and its extreme speed/size is the trade. Owner-listenable evidence
(committed): `spikes/g4-feasibility/narration/samples/` — `piper-en_US-amy-low.wav`,
`say-Samantha.wav`, `espeak-ng-sent{0..3}.wav` (total ~1.9 MB). Subjective selection is the
owner's audition at 6.6 admission time, against the licence verdicts above.
