# Learners use all required subjects and activity formats

## Description

Extend the existing learning/assessment experience with functioning content in every required subject/activity family and other-domain learner-directed generation. Apply the same breadth to the completed instructor approval shell. Own the assignment's AI Fluency presentation alongside these working demonstrations, not a standalone horizontal validation epic.

## Outcome

Learners can use representative required learning experiences and self-directed topics beyond one pilot case, while the project can explain all four AI Fluency competencies with actual examples.

## Done when

1. Functioning representative mini-courses cover software-engineering/development fundamentals, effective AI coding-agent use and soft/product skills; include both non-CS and CS-background treatment without factual contradictions.
2. Text/images, interactive simulations, role play/mock interviews, mini-games, AI-narrated animation/slides and online coding each have a functioning representative activity, appropriate source/objective links and required-activity evidence. No inert placeholders or requirement that every lesson use every format; narrated/moving content has accessible alternatives.
3. Demonstrate another-domain generation and learner-created topic/path through the personal route without instructor enrollment/approval. Demonstrate instructor-mode breadth separately with exact-version approval, restricted access, inherited rubric/completion/review and derivative-data controls.
4. Present project-specific Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence examples grounded in working behavior, verification and accountability. Do not invent outcomes, require in-product 4D grading or label single-user observations as efficacy/PMF evidence.
5. Once authorized and sandbox/provider guarantees hold, production verification exercises all activity families, isolated code execution failure/abuse limits, narration alternatives, personal/instructor boundaries and rights-checked content/rubric fixtures. Each provider integration passes consent/data-lifecycle checks before activation.

## Boundaries

- Owns required-subject/format/open-domain breadth, isolated coding and narration/media touch points, representative rights-checked fixtures and R19's presentation deliverable.
- Consumes epic 2 objective/source-linked generation, epic 3 required-activity/rubric/evidence contracts and epic 5 restricted authoring/approval. Earlier identity/lifecycle/review contracts remain binding through those integrations.
- The dependency on epic 5 enables instructor-mode breadth verification, not permission to impose instructor approval on personal learning. A pilot limited to unit-testing does not replace other subjects or formats.
- Initiative closure, not another epic, executes D8's sole-participant pilot and seven-day observation after all functional gates; reuse each epic's fixtures and refresh presentation evidence with truthful closure findings. This epic does not claim the pilot already ran.
- No unrestricted user-code execution, narrated-content provider assumed approved, public instructor courses, extra study participants invented from test accounts, or test/quality cuts to make breadth fit.
