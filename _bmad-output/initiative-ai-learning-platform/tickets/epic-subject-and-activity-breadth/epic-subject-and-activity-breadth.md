# Learners use all required subjects and activity formats

## Description

Extend the existing learning/assessment experience with functioning content in every required subject area and activity family, and other-domain learner-directed generation, on the platform epics 1–5 built. Consume their contracts — never re-write them: epic 2 objective/source-linked generation, epic 3 required-activity/rubric/completion evidence, epic 4 review states (transitively) and epic 5's restricted authoring/approval path for instructor-mode demonstration. Own the isolated code-execution runner and offline narration integrations with feasibility evidence preceding any activation, the rights-checked breadth fixtures, and the assignment's AI Fluency 4D presentation alongside these working demonstrations — not as a standalone validation epic.

## Outcome

Learners can use representative required learning experiences in all three subject areas and every activity family, and self-directed topics beyond one pilot case, while the project can explain all four AI Fluency competencies with actual project examples.

## Requirements

Each line carries its parent requirement's words at this epic's altitude; local ids resolve to the parent ids in `covers` (R9 subject areas, R17 activity families, R18 paths, R19 deliverable), except R12, whose source is the initiative's touch-point rule and D7's content bar. The PRD companion's §4.6 consequences and the UX spine's activity-family rules bind the stories through References. Every family lives inside the existing lesson shell and content-item tracker, renders the honest capability-unavailable state when gated, and obeys the media floor (no autoplay, visible pause/stop, transcript, adjustable rate).

- **R1 — Software-engineering/development fundamentals** (parent R9): a functioning representative mini-course covers software-engineering/development fundamentals beyond the pilot's unit-testing case — objectives and source-linked units through epic 2's contracts, required activities and required tests through epic 3's contracts, non-CS and CS treatments without factual contradictions (consuming epic-2 adaptation). Verify a learner completes the SE-fundamentals representative course end to end and its content is demonstrably distinct from the pilot case.
- **R2 — Effective use of AI coding agents** (parent R9): a functioning representative mini-course covers effective AI coding-agent use — what to delegate, how to specify, how to verify agent output — built on the same generation/evidence contracts. Verify a learner completes the AI-coding-agent representative course end to end, including at least one activity that exercises verifying AI-generated output.
- **R3 — Soft/product skills** (parent R9): a functioning representative mini-course covers soft/product skills in software development (for example communication, prioritization or feedback), again through the shared contracts and both-background treatments. Verify a learner completes the soft-skills representative course end to end.
- **R4 — Text and images** (parent R17): text/image activities function as authored required content: required learning images are registered scoped derivatives acquired only through controlled ports, rendered via same-origin authenticated `no-store` handlers with CSP blocking unapproved remote loads and no shared-optimizer caching, with text alternatives. Verify an image-bearing required activity completes with evidence while direct and remote asset loads are blocked.
- **R5 — Interactive simulations** (parent R17): at least one functioning interactive simulation activity: deterministic declarative content with learner-manipulable inputs and observable outcomes mapped to objective revisions and typed evidence; keyboard operable with a reduced-motion path. Verify manipulating the simulation to distinct outcomes submits once and records activity evidence.
- **R6 — Role play and mock interviews** (parent R17): functioning role-play/mock-interview activities: authored scenario/turn structure with bounded branching, captured learner responses, visible transcript, disclosed rubric/feedback after submission and typed evidence mapping. Authored scripted turns are the functioning representative; live conversational AI is a separately gated enhancement, not a breadth condition.
- **R7 — Mini-games** (parent R17): functioning mini-game activities: declared deterministic goal/rules/state machine with explicit win/lose/complete outcomes, keyboard operable, no XP/streaks/engagement theater and no learner-facing time limits; outcomes map to activity-submission evidence. Verify both win and loss paths record honestly and feed completion only through the required-activity contract.
- **R8 — AI-narrated animations and slides** (parent R17): functioning narrated animation/slide activities: synchronized slide/animation sequences with offline-generated narration from approved scripts (audio as a registered scoped derivative), transcript/captions reachable from the same content item, no autoplay, visible pause/stop, adjustable rate, reduced-motion alternative — motion never the sole carrier of teaching content. Narration activates only under admitted engine/voice license evidence (G4); the text/slide sequence functions without audio.
- **R9 — Online coding in isolated execution** (parent R17): functioning online-coding activities: bounded Python pure-function exercises whose submissions execute only on a dedicated isolated runner (never the web/worker process, `eval` or `vm`), per-job no-network sandbox with enforced CPU/wall/memory/process/disk/output/concurrency limits, ephemeral filesystem destroyed after completion, host-level egress denial, server-validated receipts with deterministic approved tests, and explicit runtime error/timeout outcomes — never model-awarded scores. Admission fails closed without fresh matching admission/audit/fingerprint evidence, per the parser-container precedent.
- **R10 — Other-domain and self-directed learning** (parent R18): users generate mini-courses outside the main domain and learners create their own topics and self-learning paths through the personal route — confirm, generate, study — without instructor enrollment or approval; instructor courses are never made public and personal study never inherits course approval gates. Verify an other-domain topic and a learner-directed multi-course path work end to end with consent/lifecycle controls intact.
- **R11 — AI Fluency presentation deliverable** (parent R19): final-presentation material explains Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence with project-specific examples grounded in this project's actual working behavior, verification evidence and accountability records. No invented outcomes, no mandatory in-product 4D assessment, and single-user observations are never labeled efficacy/PMF evidence. Verify a rehearsed presentation covers all four competencies with cited project examples and stated evidence limits.
- **R12 — Rights-checked representative fixtures** (initiative Boundaries touch point; D7 content bar; parents R9/R17/R18 evidence base): every breadth fixture is original synthetic or rights-verified with recorded attribution/license/provenance; cited factual claims and answer/rubric pairs in required fixtures are manually checked against their sources; doubtful material is quarantined, never shipped. Verify the consolidated fixture registry with rights and claim-check records covering all representative content.

## Constraints

- Parent C1–C7 bind every story. **C3 is load-bearing here**: a focused slice or pilot must not silently remove required subjects, activity families, instructor workflows or other-domain/self-learning scope; delivery priorities require user agreement. All three subject areas and all six format families remain required — a disabled or inert placeholder does not satisfy R17 (D7 milestone 3).
- C4 applies to every new activity/content path: exact-version approval and enrolled-only access hold in course scope; personal study carries AI-generated/unreviewed labels without an instructor gate.
- D5 trust boundary: uploaded instructions, retrieved text and generated output can never authorize role changes, reveal another person's data or execute code; user coding runs only in an isolated, resource-bounded execution environment whose selection/testing is a mandatory implementation gate before activation.
- D7 milestone-3 bars: functioning representative activities across text/images, simulations, role play/mock interviews, mini-games, narrated animation/slides and isolated online coding — no placeholders; manually checked fixture claims/rubrics; keyboard/360px/desktop operability; narration text equivalents; honest 120-second processing states; the quality bar is never lowered to make breadth fit.
- AD-12 in full: one versioned discriminated activity contract covering all six families, each declaring objective revisions, required/optional, scored/unscored, input/output schema, accessibility equivalent and evidence mapping; renderers consume validated declarative content — AI can never supply executable HTML/JS; submissions never directly award a pass; learning assets and narration audio are registered scoped derivatives behind same-origin authenticated handlers; narration is offline by default (remote TTS needs AD-10); online coding only on dedicated isolated runner hosts.
- AD-5/AD-6 (immutable revisions; exact-version approval covers activity content and narration scripts/audio variants in course scope), AD-7 (typed activity-submission evidence), AD-8 (no direct pass from submissions), AD-10 (no blanket inherited eligibility/consent for any narration/generation provider) and AD-18 with G4: runner and narration feasibility evidence precedes activation, fail-closed, quarantine on doubt — mirroring the project's parser-sandbox admission pattern.
- Execution gates, as narrowed 2026-10-29 (see Notes): the envelope's `after` gates on epic 2 (closed); the epic-3/5 needs are contract-level, carried by the entry `after` pins (3.8 on entry 1, 5.2/5.3 on entry 10; every other entry chains through entry 1), and epic 1's contracts are reached transitively (2026-10-21 decision — no direct epic-1 pins). Only the G4 feasibility spike (entry 2) is started; it selects and activates nothing. No live provider, deployment or real-data use is authorized.
- The UX spine's deferred activity-family interaction design is settled by this refinement: the three inherited rules above plus the per-family Requirements lines are the binding interaction contracts. The static product pages under `src/app/screens` are the visual porting reference; no second visual spine.

## Done when

1. Functioning representative mini-courses cover software-engineering/development fundamentals, effective AI coding-agent use and soft/product skills; include both non-CS and CS-background treatment without factual contradictions.
2. Text/images, interactive simulations, role play/mock interviews, mini-games, AI-narrated animation/slides and online coding each have a functioning representative activity, appropriate source/objective links and required-activity evidence. No inert placeholders or requirement that every lesson use every format; narrated/moving content has accessible alternatives.
3. Demonstrate another-domain generation and learner-created topic/path through the personal route without instructor enrollment/approval. Demonstrate instructor-mode breadth separately with exact-version approval, restricted access, inherited rubric/completion/review and derivative-data controls.
4. Present project-specific Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence examples grounded in working behavior, verification and accountability. Do not invent outcomes, require in-product 4D grading or label single-user observations as efficacy/PMF evidence.
5. Once authorized and sandbox/provider guarantees hold, production verification exercises all activity families, isolated code execution failure/abuse limits, narration alternatives, personal/instructor boundaries and rights-checked content/rubric fixtures. Each provider integration passes consent/data-lifecycle checks before activation.

## Boundaries

- Owns required-subject/format/open-domain breadth, the activity-family contracts and renderers, isolated coding and narration/media integrations with their feasibility evidence, representative rights-checked fixtures and R19's presentation deliverable.
- Consumes epic 2 objective/source-linked generation, epic 3 required-activity/rubric/evidence/completion contracts and epic 5 restricted authoring/approval — via their contracts. Earlier identity/lifecycle/review contracts remain binding through those integrations; this epic adds no second scoring, progress or approval truth.
- The dependency on epic 5 enables instructor-mode breadth verification, not permission to impose instructor approval on personal learning. A pilot limited to unit-testing does not replace other subjects or formats.
- Initiative closure, not another epic, executes D8's sole-participant pilot and seven-day observation after all functional gates; it reuses this epic's fixtures and refreshes presentation evidence with truthful closure findings. This epic does not claim the pilot already ran.
- No unrestricted user-code execution, no narration provider assumed approved, no public instructor courses, no extra study participants invented from test accounts, and no test/quality cuts to make breadth fit.
