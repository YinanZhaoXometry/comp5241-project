Your data

Everything in this workspace belongs to you. Take it with you, or remove it entirely — both actions are shown here honestly, including what this sample can and cannot do.

Export your data

One JSON file containing everything the sample workspace holds for you:

  • Studies — names, goals, and the plan you confirmed
  • Materials list — titles and upload dates, not the files themselves
  • Practice history — attempts, misses, and which feedback you saw
  • Reflections — the notes you wrote on the progress screen

In this sample, export is simulated — the file contains sample data, generated on this page. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Delete everything

This removes every study, practice record, and reflection in the workspace. In this sample nothing is really stored, so nothing is really deleted — the receipt below shows what the real flow would confirm. In a live product this cannot be undone.
