New study — Privacy & data

Before this study is created, here is exactly what the AI does with your materials — and what it never does. Nothing is sent anywhere until you agree below.

01 Materials · 02 Goal & plan · 03 Privacy & data

What happens to your data

Plain-language summary. The full privacy note is linked at the bottom of this page.

The AI processes

  • Your uploaded materials — the three files from step 01, to build lessons only from them.
  • Your goal text from step 02, to shape the plan.
  • Your practice answers later, to decide what to review — never to grade you.

The AI never

  • Trains on your data — your files and answers are not used to improve any model.
  • Shares your materials with anyone, including other learners or instructors, unless you choose to.
  • Reads anything outside the files you uploaded for this study.

Where processing happens

  • On the study service hosting this app, inside your account. Files are encrypted in transit and at rest.

Retention is yours

  • Materials are kept while the study exists. Delete the study — or everything, below — and they are gone.
  • You can export a copy of all your data at any time.

Your choices

Only the first is required. The other two stay off unless you turn them on, and you can change them later in settings.
