What changed vs the current spine

  • Palette: green ink + lime/olive semantics → warm neutral ramp with a single terracotta accent. Recorded in the brief as a spine revision.
  • Type: Georgia serif display + system sans body — no webfont dependency, so CSP font-src 'self' holds without a plan change.
  • Shape: radii move from 4–6px to 8/12/16px; pills stay fully round.
  • Depth: tonal-only spine decision revisited — ring shadows everywhere, plus one whisper-soft shadow allowed on overlays only.
  • Accessibility floor unchanged: 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 borders, visible focus ring, 44px targets, 64px answer rows, reduced-motion gating.
