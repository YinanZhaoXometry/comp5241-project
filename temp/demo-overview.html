Concept overview

Learn anything with evidence, not vibes.

Fieldnotes turns the materials you already have — papers, slides, notes — into a small course you can actually finish. It drafts the plan, practices recall with you, and keeps visible proof of what stuck. No streaks, no confetti: just a record you can trust.

Open the sample study How the loop works

The learning loop

Four steps, repeated per study. The AI drafts and drills inside boundaries you set — it reads only the materials you give it, and it shows its working.

01

Materials

You upload sources — a paper, lecture slides, your own notes. Fieldnotes reads only these.

02

Mini-course

A short course is drafted from your materials — a goal and a plan you confirm before anything is scheduled.

03

Practice & recall

Short sessions pull knowledge back out of memory — the unglamorous step that makes it stick.

04

Evidence

Coverage and recall records show what you can reproduce without notes — not what you clicked through.

AI fluency, in four dimensions

Fieldnotes also tracks how well you work with AI itself. Four capabilities, stated plainly — each grows through the work, not through lectures about it.

Delegation

Deciding what to hand to the AI and what to think through yourself.

Description

Asking precisely: goals, constraints, and what a good answer looks like.

Discernment

Judging AI output against evidence instead of mistaking fluency for truth.

Diligence

Checking sources and staying honest about what you actually understand.
