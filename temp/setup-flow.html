Setup & lifecycle flow

The nine account-lifecycle states from the experience spine, U0 through U8, in the order a learner meets them. Cards link to the designed screen where one exists.

This page is a design artifact — it maps the flow; it is not itself a product screen. All copy shown is sample copy.

U0 Designed

Signed out

What the learner seesThe sign-in card: email field, one continue button, and a sample-workspace link. No marketing panel.

Honesty noteThe sample states plainly that no account is created and no email is sent.

Open sign-in
U1 Spine-only

Creating account

What the learner seesA short pending state after submitting the email — "setting up your workspace" — with no spinner theater beyond what the task actually takes.

Honesty noteIf creation takes longer than a few seconds, the copy says so instead of looping a fake animation.

No designed screen in this sample yet.
U2 Spine-only

First sign-in

What the learner seesA plain welcome: what Fieldnotes does, in two sentences, and where to start. No tour, no feature carousel.

Honesty noteThe welcome names the AI boundary up front — it reads only the materials you upload.

No designed screen in this sample yet.
U3 Designed

Empty library

What the learner sees"No saved studies yet" with one quiet call to create the first study. Nothing is padded with demo content to look alive.

Honesty noteAn empty library is stated as a fact, and the empty state pattern lives in the system-states gallery.

Open library
U4 Designed

First study setup

What the learner seesUpload materials, confirm the goal and plan the AI drafted, then start. Nothing is scheduled until the learner confirms.

Honesty noteThe plan is shown before it is run — the learner approves the goal, not just the upload.

Open study setup
U5 Designed

Privacy consent

What the learner seesThe AI-processing consent panel: what is read, what is sent, and what stays local — in plain language, before the first generation.

Honesty noteConsent is a real gate, not a banner: the study does not start until the learner has read and chosen.

Open privacy & data
U6 Designed

Optional starting check

What the learner seesThree self-rating questions — not a test — that suggest a starting lesson. Skipping is a first-class choice, shown as clearly as continuing.

Honesty noteThe result is a suggestion with the reasoning attached; the learner decides, and can redo it later.

Open starting check
U7 Designed

Export

What the learner seesA settings panel listing exactly what an export contains and its format, with a prepare-then-download flow.

Honesty noteIn the sample the export is simulated and says so; in product, export is a standing right, not a retention hurdle.

Open export & deletion
U8 Designed

Deletion receipt

What the learner seesAfter a two-step confirm, a quiet receipt: what was deleted, when, and a reference code. No guilt copy, no "we'll miss you".

Honesty noteThe receipt is the only confirmation — deletion cannot be undone, and the copy says exactly that.

Open export & deletion
