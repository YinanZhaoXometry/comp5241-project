System states

Every state pattern in the system, shown on the library screen as the host context. The rule across all of them: say what is true, say what still works, and never decorate a wait.

Loading

Skeleton rows match the real row shape so content doesn't jump when it arrives. The pulse animation collapses to static under prefers-reduced-motion; loading copy always says what is loading.

library.html · while studies load — "Loading your saved studies…"

Empty

Empty states state the fact, then offer one quiet next step. They never pad the screen with sample content to look alive, and they never blame the learner.

library.html · state U3 — no studies yet

No saved studies yet

When you create a study it will appear here with its plan and evidence. Starting empty is the normal beginning, not a problem.

Create your first study

Error

Recoverable errors use the warm amber plate — never alarming red, which is reserved for destructive actions. Copy says what happened in plain language, what was kept safe, and offers one retry.

library.html · studies failed to load

Your saved studies didn't load.

Nothing was lost — the list just couldn't be fetched. Your studies are stored safely; this is a reading problem, not a saving problem.

Partial / offline-honest

The offline banner names exactly what still works and what is paused — it never pretends to be fully online, and it never overstates the outage. It sits above the content without blocking it.

library.html · while offline

Offline. You're offline — showing your saved studies. Practice and AI features are paused until you reconnect; anything you read here is complete and current as of your last connection.

Confirm dialog

For reversible-but-disruptive actions. Focus moves into the dialog on open, Esc closes it, and focus returns to the button that opened it. One primary action inside — the confirming verb.

library.html · pausing a study's reminders

Danger zone

Quiet by design: a thin crimson border and an outlined danger button — never a filled red page. Two-step confirm, and danger is never styled like the terracotta primary CTA. The full pattern lives on the owner export screen.

library.html · removing a study (sample)

Remove this study

Removing a study deletes its plan, practice history, and reflections from this workspace. In a live product this cannot be undone.

Sample only — nothing was removed. In a live product the study would be gone and a confirmation would appear here.
